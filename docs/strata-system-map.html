1D · ⚠ Methylation RESOLUTION — gene-level now, promoter-level as the upgrade

Under review 2026-06-05 · possible pre-Saturday change · the most important data caveat

What we have (and who did it): GDSC methylation is provider-imputed, gene-level — one β per gene (~14,608), distributed that way by Cell Model Passports (…imputed_methylation.csv; columns are gene symbols). The assay is probe-level (~450k CpG sites); CMP collapsed probes→genes and imputed missing values. We did NOT aggregate or impute it — we consume it as-is. (Our only imputation anywhere is a light per-probe mean-fill on the probe-level TCGA matrix, §1B.)

Why it matters: the methylation that regulates a gene sits at its promoter CpG island, and promoter vs gene-body can differ or oppose; averaging the whole gene into one number washes out the promoter-specific signal. This is precisely why MGMT→temozolomide is null here (a promoter event); CDKN2A/MTAP survived because their signal is strong enough to persist through the averaging.

Upgrade path → promoter resolution (verify current files first): CCLE RRBS promoter methylation (DepMap; ~843 lines × ~17,182 promoter regions) joined to GDSC drug AUC or DepMap PRISM by cell-line ID; or a GEO Illumina 450K cell-line series. rank_response_markers is feature-agnostic — point it at a promoter/probe β-matrix and the same Spearman+FDR scan runs at higher resolution. Cost: more features, fewer lines (~843 for CCLE), a cell-line ID join. Payoff: likely recovers MGMT→TMZ and sharpens every marker.

2 Inference rules

THE core discovery rule — rank_response_markers

For each of the ~14,608 genes: spearmanr(gene β, outcome AUC) over shared non-NaN samples (n ≈ 960) → Benjamini-Hochberg FDR → keep q < 0.1 → sort by |rho| desc. Returns [gene, rho, pvalue, qvalue, n].

Sign: negative rho = methylation marks sensitivity; positive = resistance. It is a hypothesis-generating screen — it selects on the outcome (see rails). Outcome-agnostic: swap drug AUC for survival/toxicity and it is the same rule.

  • discover_responder_subgroup — median-split lines on the top marker's β (1=high, 0=low) → test_drug_association. Turns the continuous marker into an operational responder subgroup.
  • test_drug_association — Kruskal-Wallis across cluster AUC distributions; responder = min-mean-AUC cluster; effect = median(AUC) − responder mean; graded.
  • score_responder thresholds — delta = median − cluster mean: ++ >0.20 · + >0.07 · neutral [−0.07,0.07] · − <−0.07 · −− <−0.20. (Real split effects ~0.03–0.05 → usually reads neutral by design.)
  • select_variable_features + cluster_samples — top-variance features + KMeans (auto-k by silhouette, k∈[2,6]). NOT discovery — a lineage detector, used only for the UMAP landscape.
  • differential_cpgs — per-gene Mann-Whitney subgroup-vs-rest for driver lists. annotate_cpgs is identity (feature = gene symbol).
  • test_survival_association — multivariate log-rank + per-cluster KM. Currently fed unsupervised clusters → null. Marker-driven survival scan not yet built.

The agent (loop.py + tools.py)

Four JSON-safe tools (list_drugs, rank_markers, evaluate_subgroup, write_opportunity_brief). Protocol: list_drugs → rank_markers → pick the most interpretable top marker (prefer known biology, e.g. CDKN2A/MTAP for CDK4/6i) → evaluate_subgroup → write brief. Two paths, identical output shape: _run_llm (Anthropic tool-use, claude-opus-4-8, MAX_TURNS=10) and _run_scripted (no key). Any API error → auto-fallback to scripted, never crashes. _KNOWN_CONTROL_GENES={CDKN2A, MTAP} fires the "recovers known biology" framing.

Essence: for a given outcome, correlate every gene's methylation against it, FDR-rank the markers, cut the responder subgroup at the top marker, agent narrates + writes it up — with a positive control recovering known biology as the trust anchor.

4 Epistemic rails — the load-bearing part

These must survive a hostile reviewer
  1. Trust comes from recovering known biology, not the subgroup p. The screen recovers CDKN2A/MTAP → Palbociclib cold (q=7.8e−25). That licenses the novel hits.
  2. The screen selects on the outcome → the median-split p is CIRCULAR — not independent validation. Always lead with the continuous Spearman rho / q-value; the split is just the operational cut. Novel hits = hypotheses.
  3. Effect sizes are small (~0.03–0.05 ΔAUC); grades read "neutral" despite tiny p (inflated by n≈960). Never headline the split effect.
  4. Methylation is gene-level imputed (provider-side), not CpG-island. Consequence: MGMT→temozolomide is NULL (rho=−0.018, p=0.58) — promoter-island silencing is averaged out. That is why the control is CDKN2A/MTAP, not MGMT. → resolution upgrade path in §1D (under review, possibly pre-Saturday).
  5. Unsupervised clustering is a lineage detector (blood vs solid), not the discovery axis.
  6. The TCGA survival split is an honest NULL (log-rank p=0.84). Kept as a real-patient landscape, never claimed as a result.
  7. GDSC = lab cell lines, public data — proof-of-method, not a clinical product. Novel hits are leads to test.
  8. A future survival / any-outcome marker scan inherits the same leakage — frame it exploratory; validate held-out/external before any claim.
  9. Don't conflate tissue compartments. Tumor (PD), liver (PK), and blood (host/immune) methylomes are largely tissue-specific and not interchangeable; blood is a poor proxy for liver/tumor at regulatory loci. GDSC = tumor-only. In a clinical cohort a subgroup is a correlation, not a mechanism, without metadata — see §7.

7 Tissue compartments & mechanistic attribution

DNA methylation is strongly tissue-specific — one compartment's methylome is a poor proxy for another's. Blood↔liver concordance is locus-dependent (a minority of genetically-driven CpGs track; regulatory CpGs do not). Pan-tissue clocks work only because they use a curated cross-tissue-stable CpG set. For drug metabolism, CYP450/UGT/transporter regulation is hepatic and is not readable from peripheral blood.

CompartmentMethylation governsRole in the drug-response chainIn our data?
Tumorcell-intrinsic sensitivity (MGMT, CDKN2A…)Pharmacodynamics — does the target respond✓ GDSC cell lines
Liverdrug-metabolizing enzymes (CYP450, UGT), transportersPharmacokinetics — how much drug reaches the target✗ hepatic-only
Blood / immunehematopoiesis, immune state, CHIP, inflammationHost context — immune-mediated efficacy + toxicity✗ (the PB thesis)

GDSC is the tumor compartment only — one clean compartment, no PK/host confound; that is why it is a clean proof-of-method. A real clinical cohort mixes all three (blood draw = immune cells; cfDNA = tumor + normal; biopsy = tumor + stroma), so a responder subgroup could be driven by any compartment — or a confound (age, cell composition, genotype/mQTL, batch).

Principle

Clinical Cohort Response Subgroups Require Metadata for Mechanistic Attribution.

A methylation-defined response subgroup is, on its own, a black-box correlation. Going from which subgroup responds to why requires metadata that attributes the signal to a compartment and mechanism: tissue source · cell-type deconvolution · genotype/mQTL · age/clock · mutation/CNV · treatment · batch. Without it you have a predictor, not a mechanism — and nothing safe to act on clinically. The engine finds the subgroup; the metadata layer earns the attribution. (Turns the limitation into the roadmap — what to collect — and aligns with the paired-design Mol-CBC architecture.)
