MethylGKB · Strata v2 · cross-indication opportunity engine

Start from the molecular state, not the disease.

As-built reference · 2026-06-06 · live: methylkb.vercel.app/strata-v2.html

What this is. v1 found a methylation-defined responder subgroup for one drug, inside one indication — which pharma already does. v2 ranks methylation-defined drug-response states that hold ACROSS the indication taxonomy — mechanism-anchored, tissue-confound-controlled, and evidence-graded — to surface opportunities the expert-gated, indication-organized review is structurally least equipped to find. Research-use, GDSC cell-line proof-of-method.

1 The reframe

Expert review is organized by indication and drug class; the highest-value opportunities are the ones that cut across those boxes — a state hidden inside a heterogeneous indication, or a mechanism connecting two conditions thought unrelated. So: stop organizing by disease taxonomy, organize by molecular state. Then a "hidden subgroup" and a "cross-indication connection" fall out of the same structure: the same methylation state recurring across different diseases is a repurposing-shaped opportunity. The v1 methylation→response engine becomes the atom; the product is the search over molecular states that cross the taxonomy.

2 The method

Two rules, applied per drug

1 · Tissue-confound safeguard. A marker→drug signal is tissue-agnostic only if it survives within-tissue (per-cancer-type) AND tissue adjustment (within-tissue-residualized correlation, r_adj). Pooled significance alone may be lineage — proven: blind clustering recovers lineage, and a naive all-gene tissue-adjusted scan is non-specific (R1b).

2 · Mechanism anchor. Don't scan all ~14,600 genes (methylation co-correlation makes that meaningless). Restrict each drug's search to its own mechanism genes — GDSC's `PUTATIVE_TARGET` (87% of drugs) + a curated `PATHWAY_NAME`→genes map (100%, tumor-suppressor-emphasized; zero external dependency). Every hit is interpretable by construction.

Evidence ladder — grade every opportunity

L0 pooled association  ·  L1 interpretable / known biology  ·  L2 survives tissue adjustment
L3 tissue-agnostic — consistent across ≥3 cancer types  ·  L4 mechanism path  ·  L5 external support  ·  L6 clinical

v1 ≈ L1. v2 targets L2–L3; L4 (mechanism) is annotation, not proof. Novel leads are screen-derived hypotheses.

4 How it was validated (the research pass)

  • R1 — tissue-confound probe: MTAP/CDKN2A→palbociclib SURVIVES within-tissue + adjustment (60–74% retained, sig in 5 tissues) = genuinely tissue-agnostic. The thesis is real.
  • R1b — naive all-gene tissue-adjusted ranking REJECTED: ~8k of 14.6k genes co-significant, obscure top markers (methylation co-correlation). → forced the mechanism anchor.
  • R2 — deep-research (cited): novelty = combination only; annotation SOLVED (GDSC's own columns); positive control is confirmation; published corroboration of the confound (PMC7906305).
  • R3a — the mechanism-anchored engine + the results above.

5 Novelty & honesty rails

Must survive a hostile reviewer
  1. Novelty is the combination/framing only — methylation-state ranked across the taxonomy + mechanism-anchored + provenance. NOT novel: methylation-as-feature, the biology, or the tissue-agnostic paradigm. Cite Comms Biol 2023 (does GDSC methylation→drug but cancer-type-stratified) and CellMinerCDB (methylation×drug but per-query).
  2. The positive control is CONFIRMATION, not discovery (CDKN2A→palbociclib published). It licenses the novel leads; the leads are hypotheses.
  3. Tissue-agnostic ≠ cross-compartment. This is tumor cell-line, gene-level methylation — not blood / liver / immune / PK / patient, and not clinical decision support.
  4. "Graph" is roadmap. v2 ships an opportunity map / ranked table, not a knowledge graph with traversal.
  5. Mechanism cards are annotation, not proof of causality. Median-split / single-tissue effects are not independent validation; the discovery statistic is the tissue-adjusted association.

6 Built vs roadmap

Built + deployed (additive; v1 untouched): the mechanism-anchored, tissue-agnostic, evidence-graded engine; the opportunity table; the demo page (strata-v2.html) with agent transcript, featured cards + live per-tissue recurrence panels, mechanism cards, negative control, evidence ladder. Scripts: v2_tissue_confound_probe.py, v2_mechanism_anchored.py, generate_v2_demo.py. 24/24 tests pass.

Roadmap (pitch as horizon, not built): multi-omic molecular state (+ expression / mutation / CNV); a real knowledge graph + autonomous traversal; external/orthogonal validation (PRISM, CTRP, CCLE); patient-cohort + compartment attribution; the federated claims universe.
