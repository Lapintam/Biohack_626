How to read the report
ρ  (methylation ↔ AUC) negative = hypermethylation marks sensitivity (lower AUC = more response)  ·  positive = marks resistance
direction label the sensitive / resistant tag already does the sign math — read it, not the sign
annotation score positive = predicted sensitive (sign-flipped for readability — opposite of ρ)
evidence level L3 GDSC only  ·  L4 + 1 external screen  ·  L5 all 3 (GDSC + PRISM + CTRP). Every lead is already response-associated and functionally silenced.

§04 — Patient methylation annotation

Drop in a methylation profile; read predicted drug sensitivities.

Hand the engine a promoter-methylation profile and it scores every validated lead against the patient — aggregating weak single-gene signals into a per-drug sensitive / resistant call with the driver genes that pushed it. Research-use enrichment and triage, not a clinical recommendation.
